# Seamless Loop Reel

Read `../references/h3-production-contract.md` before authoring.

Create replayable reels built around one visual mechanism that returns every visible state to its exact opening condition.

## Loop lock

Define the loop object, environment, camera path, initial composition, initial object state, light state, particle state, and sound cycle. Choose one mechanism: circular camera move, reversible transformation, occlusion reset, match-motion portal, repeated physical action, or environmental cycle. The mechanism must be physically readable and achievable within the selected duration.

## Five-second topology

Map opening lock, escalation, concealed reset, and exact return. The final frame must match the first in camera position, focal length, framing, subject pose, object placement, lighting, color, and motion direction. Hide the reset behind a foreground wipe, full-frame occlusion, rotational symmetry, or a naturally repeating motion. Maintain constant motion cadence across the seam.

## Audio loop

Design ambience, rhythm, and foley whose tail joins its head without a click or unresolved swell. Avoid dialogue unless the final syllable and breath pattern are explicitly authored for looping.

## QA

Compare first and last frames directly. Reject exposure jumps, mismatched parallax, changed object counts, discontinuous shadows, camera drift, particle discontinuity, or a final pause that reveals the seam. The formatter may refine the reset mechanism but cannot change the locked opening/final topology.
