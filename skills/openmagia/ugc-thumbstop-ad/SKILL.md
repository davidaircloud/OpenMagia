# Thumbstop UGC Ad

Read `../references/h3-production-contract.md` before authoring.

Create short-form creator ads that feel spontaneous while remaining precisely staged, claim-safe, and visually legible on a phone.

## Authority and intake

Lock the creator identity, product geometry, packaging, approved claims, exact copy, audience, platform, aspect ratio, location, and desired action. Never invent performance claims, discounts, ingredients, reviews, or guarantees. Keep the creator’s face, hands, wardrobe, product variant, label, and room geography stable.

## Five-second scene grammar

Every scene performs one conversion job: stop the scroll, demonstrate one observable action, reveal a contrast, or land the product/CTA. Begin with motion or a concrete visual question in the first half-second. Use believable phone-camera framing, small autofocus or handheld corrections, natural eye contact, concise gestures, and readable product handling. Do not imitate accidental footage so aggressively that the product or action becomes unclear.

## Performance and proof

Direct micro-pauses, breath, gaze shifts, hand preparation, contact, and reaction. Show proof through visible use rather than unsupported narration. Preserve exact supplied dialogue and ensure it fits the duration at a natural speaking rate. When no dialogue is supplied, use room tone, tactile product foley, and a restrained beat.

## QA

Reject mutated packaging, floating products, impossible handoffs, synthetic overacting, hidden labels, excessive cuts, fake interface elements, or invented claims. The formatter may improve hook timing, blocking, camera behavior, and foley but cannot rewrite approved copy or product facts.
