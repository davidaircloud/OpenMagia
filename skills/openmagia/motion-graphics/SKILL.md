# Motion Graphics Director

Read `../references/h3-production-contract.md` before authoring.

Design kinetic typography and graphic animation in which every element has a hierarchy, timing purpose, and readable state.

## Intake

Use the refinement sheet to collect exact copy, line breaks, hierarchy, language, logo/assets, palette, typography character, visual references, aspect ratio, duration, platform safe areas, and audio/beat cues. Never paraphrase or “correct” approved copy without permission.

Ask the user to choose or let the local formatter recommend a visual system: editorial grid, modular cards, type-as-object, dimensional broadcast graphics, collage/type, data-driven geometry, or minimal title sequence. Resolve whether the scene supports a subject/product or is fully graphic.

## Build the motion system

Define the grid, margins, scale steps, type roles, shape family, line weight, texture, depth model, color states, and one transition motif. Assign one visual owner at a time. Secondary layers may follow with a deliberate delay but cannot compete with the active message.

Create a copy ledger containing the exact string, entry time, fully readable time, minimum hold, exit time, position, scale, and contrast state. Keep essential copy inside the target safe composition. Long copy must be split into sequential messages rather than shrunk or flashed.

## H3 beat map

Plan at 24 fps within 15 seconds. Each beat needs exact time, active copy/asset, starting arrangement, motion path, transformation rule, impact/settle, outgoing geometry, and sound accent. Transitions must be motivated by shared edges, masks, paths, subject motion, or matched geometry—not arbitrary wipes.

Describe generated typography conservatively: stable short phrases, sufficient hold, no simultaneous mutation, and no text hidden behind subjects. When exact brand typography or logos matter, use supplied assets as references and preserve geometry.

The local formatter may improve timing language, transition mechanics, camera/depth, and sound synchronization. It may not change copy, spelling, hierarchy, brand marks, palette locks, duration, or reference roles. OpenMagia then canonicalizes H3 fields and cut ranges.

## QA and recovery

Review each message at normal playback speed for hierarchy, contrast, line breaks, safe area, hold duration, and transition ownership. Confirm the final hero frame is readable without motion. If type flickers or mutates, reduce words and motion; if chaotic, remove secondary layers; if generic, strengthen the grid and transition motif; if off-brand, return to verified assets and palette.
