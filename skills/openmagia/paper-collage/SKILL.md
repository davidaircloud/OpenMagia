# Paper Collage Explainer

Read `../references/h3-production-contract.md` before authoring.

Turn a learning point, opinion, or narration into tactile editorial paper collage with factual clarity and visible construction.

## Intake

Use the refinement sheet to resolve the single learning goal, audience, source facts, narration/exact copy, desired takeaway, duration, aspect ratio, palette, visual references, and audio/subtitle preference. If the topic has several claims, ask the user to prioritize or split them into scenes.

When a metaphor is not supplied, have the local formatter propose two or three physically buildable collage concepts and explain what each communicates. Ask for a choice when the concepts materially change meaning; otherwise select the clearest and surface it in the preview.

## Visual grammar

Define paper stock, fibers, torn versus cut edges, halftone scale, print registration, ink density, adhesive/tape, shadow depth, tabletop or rostrum lighting, limited palette, and camera orientation. References can lock texture, palette, object shapes, composition, or print language; state the role of each.

Create an asset list of backgrounds, cut characters/objects, labels, arrows, diagrams, joints, and replacement pieces. Every object must remain visibly made from paper. Avoid glossy CG, liquid morphing, perfect vector edges, depth inconsistent with layered sheets, or unexplained substitution.

## Explain through motion

Build a factual sequence: introduce the question/state, construct the metaphor, demonstrate the relationship or mechanism, then resolve to the takeaway. Each beat needs time range, active paper pieces, physical movement, fact represented, narration/copy, transition, and settled composition.

Use slides, hinges, folds, stacks, rotations, swaps, tears, and frame-by-frame replacement. Maintain paper contact, occlusion, shadows, and believable pivots. Keep diagrams and short text readable long enough; never let decorative texture obscure the learning point.

The local formatter may improve metaphor staging, chronological beats, camera, transitions, and tactile sound. It cannot alter source facts, exact narration/copy, or reference roles. OpenMagia validates H3 timing and fields.

## Audio, approval, and QA

Default to collage foley—paper rustle, snips, taps, tape, card slides—and no voiceover/music unless requested. Present the metaphor and beat plan before generating when factual interpretation is consequential.

Check factual mapping, material consistency, readable text, one idea per beat, coherent palette, shadows/contact, duration, and final takeaway. If the result is confusing, simplify the metaphor; if it looks digital, expose construction and irregular edges; if too busy, remove decorative layers.
